BuffPrints

Delete your BuffPrints account and data

BuffPrints is a campus walking game for CU Boulder, published on Google Play by Zach Urban. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BuffPrints and go to Progress.
  2. Tap Privacy & delete my data.
  3. Tap Delete everything and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it.

Without the app

Email a deletion request from the address you sign in with to the BuffPrints contact address with the subject “Delete my BuffPrints account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything: your email sign-in, nickname, dorm or team, claimed paths, distance, badges and streaks, weekly zone totals, hazard pins and votes, path names, and every Herd and Seek check-in, team membership and demo reward token. Nothing is kept.

Your detailed GPS route and class schedule are never uploaded, so they live only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or using Delete everything erases them.
